## About Me

Hi, I'm **Krysdal Evangelista** — a junior at **Peking University**, majoring in **Computer Science** and **Economics**.

You can also call me Ariel — that's the name I use in Spanish class. Partly because of the Disney princess, partly because of Sylvia Plath's *Ariel*.
